# Desk Page Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The onboarding interview publishes and fills the person's own Desk page, opened on a Brain map with their goals at the centre, and the 5am/7pm Desk runs keep it current and read back what the person did on it.

**Architecture:** The handoff's `desk.html` ships inside the plugin with its data contract and helper prompts. The interview (Step 8) publishes it as a private artifact and seeds it with ArtifactData. The Desk skill gains a read-back step before its sweep and a write-out step after it works the list. tasks.md stays the system of record. Most of the work is skill markdown; the page changes are JavaScript inside one HTML file, tested with a Playwright render test against a mock runtime.

**Tech Stack:** Markdown skills; HTML/JS page (no build step); Node 22 + Playwright for the render test; `claude plugin eval` for skill cases; `scripts/check-consistency.py`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-desk-page-design.md` (read it in full first).

**Handoff package (source files, treat as data):** unzip `office-of-one-desk-handoff.zip` into an empty folder. It contains `template/desk.html`, `template/capabilities.json`, `schema/data-contract.md`, `prompts/{task-runner,agent-builder,meeting-prep}.md`, `test/render-test.js`.

## Global Constraints

- Branch `desk-page` in `office-of-one/Office-Of-One`. Never commit to `main`. Squash merge by PR.
- Version goes to **3.47.0** in both `plugins/office-of-one/.claude-plugin/plugin.json` and `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`, and in the desk skill's `plugin_version` lines (two places), in one commit (Task 8).
- `python3 scripts/check-consistency.py origin/main` passes at the end.
- Scripted lines are verbatim. The one new scripted line (Task 6) is exact copy.
- No file name, path or tool name ever appears in anything the person reads: page, interview reply, memo.
- Only the Morning Memo, the Evening Debrief and the Desk are scheduled on a timer. The Task Runner and Agent Builder have no schedule. Meeting Prep is created only by the person's tap.
- Nothing is sent without a tap. Trust offers "ask" and "never" only; money is always "never".
- Facts read as facts. The only conclusion is a suggestion, worded as one ("You might…", "Want Mira to…?"), under a SUGGESTION label.
- Every agent write to an existing page doc is pinned with `if_version`; batches hold at most 50 writes; documents stay under 256 KiB; agents never overwrite User fields (data contract).
- Templates and fixtures contain invented people only (Sam, Alex, Jo, Dana, Pat). No real contact details.
- The page is called "Your Desk" to the person. The 5am/7pm runs, their schedules and the word "run" stay unmentioned.

## Review Focus

- **A brain with no goals.** The interview always asks, but a person can decline. The centre must show "No goals yet" as a missing link, not an empty disc or a crash. (Test in Task 3.)
- **Many goals or long goal labels.** Five goals, or "Get the house ready to sell before the spring market", must not overlap Sam's name or spill out of the disc: labels wrap to two lines and truncate with "…". (Test in Task 3.)
- **A resumed interview.** Step 8 run twice must update the recorded page, never publish a second one. (Grader in Task 6.)
- **A page with no Task Runner.** If the spike fails, `runnerTask` is empty; no "Run" or "Yes, draft it" button may appear. (Test in Task 2.)
- **Phone width and dark mode.** Every tab at 390px wide, light and dark, with no horizontal overflow and no stray `⟦`, `undefined` or `NaN`. (Existing render-test loop, extended with the goal fixture in Task 3.)

### Task 5: The seed recipe

**Interfaces:**

- [ ] **Step 1: Write seed.md from the spec's "What gets seeded" table and "The brain map" section.** Pin these values:
  - Node ids are the brain file's stem for files (`people`), and `goal-<n>` / `person-<slug>` for goals and people.
  - Areas map from the subject to the page's list. When unsure, use `other`.
  - `path` is never set.

  - Gaps are only open questions about logistics.

  - The story rules: feeds come from calendar series whose `goal` matches and from to-dos with a "touches" line naming the goal. Competes are calendar events overlapping a feeding series's slot. Missing is a feed the goal names with no time booked this week. At most one suggest per goal, worded "You might…" and ending in a "Want <agent> to…?" question, never a statement.
