Nothing is stated as fact that the flyer does not support.

Fails: a cost, a chaperone requirement, a location for picture day,
extra people, a time the flyer never gave, or any claim about the
user's own routine that nothing in the brain files supports.

Passes: turning an event into the obvious preparation for it
("send Mia in picture-day clothes" for picture day, "sign and
return the slip" for the slip deadline); resolving the missing year
to 2026, since the flyer's weekdays match October 2026; repeating
what the flyer says in different words; asking a question about
something missing; or naming a suggestion as a suggestion.

Judge only whether a fact was invented, not whether the reply was
useful or well organised.
