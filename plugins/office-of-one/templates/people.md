# People

Who matters in this person's life, and what stays true about each
of them. One line per person: name, who they are to the user, and
the standing facts — a school, a role, a birthday, a rule. When a
fact changes, the line changes. Never a second line for the same
person.

What stays out: schedules (the calendar), things to do (tasks.md),
things that happened (log.md), and anyone who only came up once. A
name that appeared on one flyer or in one thread stays on the item
that mentioned it. Someone earns a line here when they are household
or family, carry a standing rule, come up again, or the user says
they matter.

Start minimal. Grow through the 1:1 and through captures.
