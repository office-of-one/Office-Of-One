# Log

The last four weeks' record. One line per day, newest first,
describing what happened in this person's life or work.

Written by: memorialize, as things happen during a session — not at
the end of one. Each Desk run also fills in yesterday's line if a
session was interrupted before it could be written.

Each week the Desk moves lines older than four weeks, word for word,
into archive/, and once a month has fully left, sums it up in
archive.md.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD — one sentence.`
