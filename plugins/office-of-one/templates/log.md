# Log

The current month's record. One line per day, newest first,
describing what happened in this person's life or work.

Written by: memorialize, as things happen during a session — not at
the end of one. The daily brief also fills in yesterday's line if a
session was interrupted before it could be written.

At the start of each month, the previous month's lines are condensed
into archive.md and removed from here.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD — one sentence.`
