# Archive

The long memory. One block per month, newest first, 5 to 10 lines
each: what changed in this person's life that month — decisions,
people, commitments made or dropped.

Written by: memorialize, at the start of each new month, by
condensing the previous month out of log.md.

Months older than six months get compressed further, to one or two
lines, keeping only what has lasting weight.

Format:

## 2026-08
- ...
- ...
