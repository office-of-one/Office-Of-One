# Archive

The long memory. One block per month, newest first, 5 to 10 lines
each: what changed in this person's life that month — decisions,
people, commitments made or dropped.

Written by: the Desk's weekly archive, once a month has fully left
log.md. The word-for-word detail behind each month is in the
archive/ folder, one file per month.

Months older than six months get compressed further, to one or two
lines, keeping only what has lasting weight. The detail stays in
archive/.

Format:

## 2026-08
- ...
- ...
