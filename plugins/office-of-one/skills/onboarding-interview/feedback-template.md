# Feedback Email Template

Used by the EMAIL test in setup-check, during onboarding Step 6 and
whenever the user says "Test my setup". Drafting and showing this
email is NOT sending it. Always compose and display the draft; only
the send is gated on the user's yes.

## Before drafting

Ask these three questions, one at a time, exactly as written. Write
each answer as given, with names and places taken out. If the user
skips one, write "skipped".

"**What worked well for you in setup?**"
"**And was anything confusing?**"
"**Anything else you'd like the Office of One team to know?**"

## The email

**To:** support@officeofone.ai
**Subject:** Setup report — [agent name] (Office of One [version])

---

Hi Office of One team,

Here's the setup report from my agent, [agent name].

SETUP AT A GLANCE
Plugin version: [version]
Time in setup: [about N minutes]
Connected: [kinds of tools][, [n] switched off]
Pre-work pasted: [yes / no]
Using it for: [work / personal / work and personal]

THE JOURNEY
1. Name me: [what happened]
2. Catch me up on your AI convos: [what happened]
3. Confirm what's connected: [what happened]
4. Schedule my tasks: [what happened]
5. Help me fill in the gaps: [what happened]
6. Test me: see Setup tests

WHERE IT WENT OFF SCRIPT
- [one line each, or "Nothing. Setup ran as written."]

GAPS
- [one line each, or "None found."]

SETUP TESTS
Calendar: [PASS / FAIL and a one-line reason]
Morning Memo: [PASS / FAIL and a one-line reason]
Friday 1:1: [PASS / FAIL and a one-line reason]
Email: this message

WHAT WAS SET UP
To-dos found: [n] · Topic files started: [n] · Questions saved for the 1:1: [n]

KIND OF HELP NEEDED
[tags]

IN MY WORDS
What worked well: [answer]
What was confusing: [answer]
Anything else: [answer]

Sent with my permission from my own account. Nothing personal is included.

---

## What each section holds

- Time in setup: only when the session shows it. Otherwise leave
  the line out. Never estimate.
- Connected: the kinds of tools, never account names or addresses.
- The journey: one short line per step, such as done as written,
  cut short, resumed after a break, or not reached.
- Where it went off script: every place the session left the
  interview guide. The user asked something the script doesn't
  cover, skipped or declined something, or asked to move on; a tool
  or scheduled task failed; a fallback line was used; or there was
  no written wording and the agent improvised.
- Gaps: what setup couldn't learn or do. Question areas not
  covered, a missing mail or calendar connector, a failed test and
  why, how many calendar contradictions were left for the Morning
  Memo, and anything the instructions didn't cover.
- What was set up: counts only.
- When the EMAIL test runs from "Test my setup" rather than the
  interview, leave out Time in setup, Pre-work pasted, The journey
  and Where it went off script. Put "Sent from a setup check, not
  the interview." under the greeting.

## Keeping it general

- Describe the kind of thing, never the thing itself: "a weekly
  kids' activity", never a child's name, the activity or the place.
- Never include names, employers, schools, cities or places, anyone's
  email address, dates of events, or quotes from mail, the calendar
  or the brain files.
- Nothing sensitive, not even in general terms: health,
  relationships, money, legal matters, life events, or anything the
  user asked to keep private. Leave that line out instead.
- If a line could only describe one person, rewrite it more
  generally or leave it out.
- The user's own answers follow the same rules.

## The tags

Three to five short tags describing the KIND of help this person
needs. Open vocabulary, not a fixed list. Rules:

- Lowercase, hyphenated, two words maximum.
- Describe the kind of help, never the person.
- No proper nouns: no names, employers, schools, or places.
- Nothing about health, relationship status, or money.
- Nothing the user asked to keep private.

Examples, not a whitelist:
kids-logistics · school-calendar · elder-care · heavy-email ·
multi-calendar · solo-household · shift-work · travel-heavy ·
volunteer-load · side-business

A tag that could only describe one person is too specific. Rewrite
it more generally or leave it out.
